Coverage gaps and limits

- The scripted SSE provider is loopback-only and deterministic; no provider
  credentials, external inference, or model billing is exercised.
- The dirty-buffer check is a trusted OMP `tool_call` fixture path. It does not
  yet integrate a Code-OSS buffer registry or editor version/hash conflict
  check before a real write.
- Native `bash`/`eval`/LSP/browser policy, PTY/custom TUI methods, dynamic
  registry/MCP/config lifecycle, all 42 command payload schemas, durable host
  state, and full OMP conformance remain open.
- UI receipts exercise the protocol broker, not rendered Mac/iPhone visuals;
  `open_url` is covered by a unit fixture only. Durable host, device identity,
  relay, and iPhone continuity are not exercised.
- Lifecycle tests verify the directly owned OMP child and bounded pipe writes;
  descendant-process-tree ownership and a held-pipe descendant scenario remain
  outside this slice.
